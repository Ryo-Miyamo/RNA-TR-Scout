# Within-repeat discordance origin contract v0.1.0

Mismatch, insertion, deletion, normalized interruption intervals, purity, exact-sequence LPS, and error-aware inferred LPS remain separate.

Mismatch/indel are alignment operations. A normalized interruption is a structured motif-breaking interval. Neither low purity nor an edit operation alone proves a biological interruption.

`discordance_origin_status` defaults to `NOT_ASSESSED` in Stage 14K. Purity threshold alone is forbidden as an origin classifier.

Biological promotion requires recurrent molecule-level support and/or replicate, cross-platform, same-molecule SNP-phasing, matched-DNA, raw-signal, or orthogonal evidence. Until truth-bearing validation exists, use `NOT_ASSESSED` or `UNRESOLVED`.
